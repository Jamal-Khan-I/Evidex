Face Verification

Optional extra layer, entirely client-side — nothing leaves your browser. Enroll your face plus a PIN once; the site will then ask for both before Pause, Unpause, or Admin Transfer actions. The PIN exists because face matching alone can't reliably tell apart people who look very similar (e.g. twins/siblings) — that's a known limit of face recognition generally, not just this build. This is a UX safeguard against someone else using an unlocked wallet, not a cryptographic control — it can't be enforced by the smart contract itself, and enrollment is per-browser, not portable across devices.

7. BRIEF FACTS OF THE CRIME ("DIGITAL ARREST" RACKET):
   On 08/02/2026, the victim received an automated IVR call claiming to be from the Department of Telecommunications (DoT) alleging 17 illegal SIM cards registered under his Aadhaar were used in money laundering and narcotics smuggling. 
   The call was transferred via Skype video conferencing to a simulated police station control room with suspects donning counterfeit IPS uniforms, displaying forged CBI badges, and showing simulated arrest warrants bearing fabricated Supreme Court digital watermarks.
   The complainant was placed under continuous 96-hour coercive "Virtual Custody" ("Digital Arrest"), prohibited from disconnecting the video feed or contacting family. Under immense psychological coercion and threat of immediate incarceration, the victim was forced to liquidate mutual funds and fixed deposits, transferring ₹4,85,00,000/- (Rupees Four Crore Eighty-Five Lakhs) across four layer-1 mule bank accounts, subsequently bridged to Tether (USDT) cryptocurrency exchanges.

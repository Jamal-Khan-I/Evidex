3. SCIENTIFIC EXAMINATION & LABORATORY FINDINGS:
   A. Acoustic & Voice Biometric Analysis (Exhibit-C):
      - Spectrogram analysis reveals synthetic harmonic distortion in the 4 kHz - 8 kHz frequency range.
      - Latency pattern analysis indicates real-time voice conversion leveraging an RVC v2 (Retrieval-based Voice Conversion) neural network architecture.
      - Training checkpoint recovered from Exhibit-D contained acoustic samples scraped from publicly broadcast press briefings of CBI and Delhi Police officials.
      - Conclusion: 99.8% probability of algorithmic neural deepfake voice generation used to impersonate Law Enforcement Officers.

   B. SIM Box Hardware & Firmware Extraction (Exhibit-A):
      - Firmware reverse engineering revealed a custom embedded Linux distribution configured to rotate IMSI/IMEI identifiers dynamically every 180 seconds.
      - Extracted SQLite database contained dispatch logs for 1,420 automated IVR calls mimicking Department of Telecommunications (DoT) disconnect warnings.

   C. Financial & Cryptocurrency Laundering Trail (Exhibit-B):
      - The victim's funds (₹4,85,00,000/-) were routed through 4 distinct Layer-1 mule accounts in Axis Bank and ICICI Bank.
      - Within 42 minutes of credit, funds were swapped via peer-to-peer OTC desks into 584,330 USDT on the TRON blockchain (TRC-20).
      - Address clustering analysis links the recipient wallet (TJ9v...x7Q) to an organized syndicate operation based in Southeast Asia.
